# AGENT.md

## Purpose

You are helping build the Agné Studio website.

Your job is to implement the agreed design—not redesign it.

If a design decision has not been made, ask rather than invent.

---

## General Principles

- Prefer consistency over creativity.
- Do not introduce new UI patterns without approval.
- Keep implementations clean and reusable.
- Prioritize accessibility.
- Mobile-first.
- Avoid unnecessary dependencies.
- Avoid unnecessary animations.

---

## Design

Always follow DESIGN_DECISIONS.md.

If something conflicts with previous decisions, follow the document.

Do not invent colors, spacing, typography or layouts.

---

## Success Criteria

Every implementation should feel like it belongs to the same product.

Before introducing a new UI pattern, ask:

- Does this already exist somewhere else?
- Is this simpler than the previous solution?
- Does it align with DESIGN_DECISIONS.md?

Consistency is valued over novelty.

---

## Components

Components should be reusable.

Avoid duplicated markup.

---

## Styling

Use the established spacing system.

Maintain consistent border radius.

Maintain consistent typography.

---

## Animation

Animations should support understanding.

Never exist purely for decoration.

Prefer subtle fades and movement.

Avoid parallax.

Avoid dramatic scroll effects.

---

## Before completing work

Check:

- spacing
- responsiveness
- accessibility
- consistency

---

## Things to Avoid

- Don't redesign agreed sections unless explicitly asked.
- Don't add placeholder content that wasn't requested.
- Don't introduce libraries without approval.
- Don't create unnecessary abstractions.
- Don't over-engineer components.
- Don't optimize prematurely.
- Don't make design assumptions when documentation exists.
