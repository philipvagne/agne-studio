# DESIGN_DECISIONS.md

## Design Philosophy

Thoughtful design over decoration.

Clarity over complexity.

Whitespace is intentional.

Typography carries the design.

Motion supports understanding.

---

## Brand Personality

Calm.

Confident.

Thoughtful.

Premium.

Human.

---

## Visual Direction

Minimal.

Editorial.

Modern.

High attention to detail.

---

## Animation

Subtle only.

No flashy effects.

No unnecessary motion.

---

## Components

Every component should have one clear purpose.

Avoid decorative UI.

---

## Homepage

The navigation, hero and work gallery should fit within the initial desktop viewport whenever reasonably possible.

The work gallery follows directly after the hero with only a small amount of breathing room.

Do not introduce a "Featured Work" heading. The work itself serves as the proof of Agné Studio's capabilities.

---

Pages

Home

Pricing

Contact

---

## Featured Work

The work itself is the sales pitch.

Keep supporting copy to an absolute minimum.

The website previews should communicate the quality of the work visually.

Each preview links directly to its live demo.

The focus is on presentation, not explanation.
